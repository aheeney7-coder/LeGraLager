Our Story

"Le Grá" means
with love.

Le Grá encapsulates warmth, camaraderie and the celebration of life — the same spirit we brew into every bottle. It's crafted at a craft brewery in Ireland using 100% Irish ingredients, with real, hand-picked shamrocks added during brewing for a taste that's genuinely rooted in the Emerald Isle.

Real shamrocks. Hand-picked, then brewed into every single bottle.

Our logo, the four-leaf shamrock, represents the values that anchor the Irish people — which is why some people simply call Le Grá "the Lucky Beer." It's brewed by Rye River Brewing Company, a BRC AAA+ accredited brewery outside Dublin.

The Beer

Crisp, clean, and full of subtle fruit notes.

Le Grá is brewed with 100% Irish barley and hops, finished with real shamrocks added during the brewing process — resulting in a clean, balanced pint with a crisp character and gentle fruit notes.
